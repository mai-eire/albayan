## Child pages

14. **No breadcrumbs.** — *Done: "Your family › Name".*
15. **The Overview tab is lame** — drop it, make Details the first tab. — *Done.*
16. **Counts on the tabs** (Homework, Notes, Resources…). — *Done.*
17. **Attendance as "3 / 6"** on the tab: present out of classes so far. — *Done.*
18. **A Fees tab**, like the office's card on a student, with "See details" to the family's fees page instead of "Record a payment", a tab mark, and no "Also in this family". — *Done.*
19. *Question:* should guardians be able to delete or archive a child? **Answer: no, and it isn't needed.** A child who graduates or leaves is `inactive`, which the office sets when the place ends — the family's list already says "No longer attending". Letting a parent delete a child would take attendance, fee and payment history with it, and Phase 3 task 8 puts deletion and anonymisation with the office, where the data-protection duty sits. What is missing is only that a family cannot *tell* the school a child is leaving; that is a message, not a delete.
20. *Question:* re-applying after a decline. **Answer: a new application, not a revived one** — the child is a year older, the year is different, and the old record is the evidence of what was decided. The shape: a "Apply again" button on a declined child's Application tab that opens the wizard with their details filled in and files a fresh `students` row for the current year; the office's drawer then lists that child's earlier applications. Not built — it needs the decision on whether the two rows are linked (a `previousApplicationId`) or only found by name.

## Decisions taken while doing this

- The other parents are shown by **name and relationship only**. In a separated family one parent's phone number is not the other's to hand out; the office has it.
- A notification's subject and child are **two columns on `notifications`**, not a generic entity reference: those are the only two things a notification is ever about.
- Allergies stay one text column, comma separated — that is how a teacher reads them on a register — and are edited as a list.
- Sections in the school-year drop-down use Mantine's group labels (a label with a rule under it), which is the house style for a divided list.
- The seed gained registers, homework, notifications and six two-parent families. Without them the tab counts, the "3 / 6" and the bell were all empty on a fresh database and none of this could be reviewed.
