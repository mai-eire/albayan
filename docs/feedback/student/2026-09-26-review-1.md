## Decided while doing this

- **What a guardian may change:** name, date of birth, gender, school year, home schooling, allergies, medical notes — the facts a family knows better than the office. **Not** the student ID (the school's filing), the Arabic level (the school's assessment) or the class (the school's decision). The page says which is which.
- **What a student sees of their carers:** names and what they are to them, nothing else. A child does not need their mother's Eircode from the school, and the guardians' sensitive columns stay out of the query — asserted in `lib/db/queries/family.test.ts`.
- **The phone tab bar had run out of room.** Students get no burger, so anything not in the bar was unreachable on a phone; with eight nav items that broke. The bar is now four tabs and a **More** tab that opens the drawer (DESIGN §3.1).
